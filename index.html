The cube alphabet

Learn six symbols
and you’re ready.

A letter means turn that side once. The apostrophe (′) means turn it the other way. The number 2 means turn it twice.

Clockwise means the direction the face turns when you look straight at that face. If you get lost, stop and put the cube back in the same position.

Beginner move guide

RRight side, clockwise
R′Right side, counter-clockwise
R2Right side, twice
R2′Same as R2: right side, twice
LLeft side, clockwise
L′Left side, counter-clockwise
L2Left side, twice
L2′Same as L2: left side, twice
UTop side, clockwise
U′Top side, counter-clockwise
F2Front side, twice
U2Top side, twice

R U′ means do two moves in order: turn the right side clockwise, then turn the top side counter-clockwise. Spaces separate moves; read them from left to right. R2′ and L2′ are the same as R2 and L2 because a half-turn has no direction.
